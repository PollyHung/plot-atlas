Raincloud plot

Three marks in one lane — a half violin for shape, a narrow box for the summary statistics, and jittered raw points beneath.

How to read it

  • Points: what was measured, and how much of it
  • Box: the summary you would quote
  • Half violin: the shape
  • Where the three disagree is the finding

What it hides

  • Almost nothing — its cost is horizontal space, not information

Reach for it when

  • When the distribution is the result rather than a nuisance
  • Six groups or fewer

Avoid it when

  • Many groups, small multiples, or column-width printing
R
ggplot(d, aes(group, value)) + ggrain::geom_rain()
Python
ptitprince.RainCloud(data=d, x='group', y='value')
