Bean plot

A violin outline with every observation drawn inside it as a short horizontal line, plus a bar at the group mean.

How to read it

  • The outline gives shape, the internal lines give the observations
  • Line density reveals the true sample size
  • A solid band means repeated values

What it hides

  • Little — it spends width instead of hiding things

Reach for it when

  • Small to moderate groups where the width is available

Avoid it when

  • Beyond about a hundred per group, where the lines merge into a block
