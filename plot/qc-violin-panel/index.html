QC violin panel

nFeature / nCount / percent.mt plot

The standard opening figure of a single-cell analysis: genes, counts and mitochondrial fraction per cell, with candidate filter thresholds drawn on.

How to read it

  • Read the tail, not the bulk
  • A long upper tail in counts suggests doublets
  • A high mitochondrial fraction suggests dying cells
  • The threshold lines are the decision the figure exists to support

What it hides

  • Each metric alone — cells that are only jointly anomalous stay invisible

Reach for it when

  • Choosing and documenting QC cutoffs before filtering
  • Pair it with counts against mitochondrial fraction

Avoid it when

  • Treating defaults as universal — thresholds vary by tissue and chemistry
