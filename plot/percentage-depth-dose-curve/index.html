Percentage depth dose curve

PDD curve; depth dose curve; central axis depth dose

Dose measured along the beam's central axis in a water phantom, expressed as a percentage of the maximum, against depth.

How to read it

  • Surface dose is high but not the maximum
  • Dose peaks at d-max, then falls steeply
  • R50, the 50% depth, sets the treatable range
  • The low tail past the fall-off is bremsstrahlung

What it hides

  • Everything off the central axis — this is one line
  • Field size and applicator, which shift the whole curve
  • Tissue heterogeneity; the phantom is uniform water

Reach for it when

  • Choosing an electron energy for a target depth
  • Commissioning and annual beam constancy checks

Avoid it when

  • Oblique incidence or a sloping patient surface
  • Beams passing through bone or lung
R
plot(depth, 100 * dose / max(dose), type = 'l', xlab = 'Depth (cm)')
Python
matplotlib.pyplot.plot(depth, 100 * dose / dose.max())
