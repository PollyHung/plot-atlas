Isodose curve

isodose distribution; isodose plot

Contours joining points of equal dose on one plane through the beam, labelled as a percentage of the prescribed or maximum dose.

How to read it

  • Each line joins points at one dose level
  • Line spacing at the field edge is the penumbra
  • Parallel evenly spaced lines mean a flat field
  • Contours bulging around the target show conformity

What it hides

  • The third dimension — one plane of a volume
  • Dose between contours, which is interpolated
  • How much tissue sits at each dose level

Reach for it when

  • Checking field placement and penumbra on a plan
  • Teaching how beam arrangement shapes dose

Avoid it when

  • Judging organ overdose — read the DVH instead
  • Plans whose hot spot lies off this plane
