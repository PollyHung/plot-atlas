Gamma index map

gamma analysis map; gamma evaluation

A per-point comparison of measured against planned dose, folding a dose-difference and a distance-to-agreement tolerance into one dimensionless number.

How to read it

  • Gamma at or below 1 passes
  • Colour shows how far past tolerance a point is
  • Failures cluster where the dose gradient is steep
  • Always read the criterion and the dose threshold with it

What it hides

  • Which of the two criteria failed
  • The sign of the error — hot and cold look alike
  • Absolute dose; everything is scaled to tolerance

Reach for it when

  • Patient-specific IMRT and VMAT quality assurance
  • Comparing a delivered fraction against its plan

Avoid it when

  • Accepting a plan on passing rate alone
  • Low-dose regions, where the threshold drives the result
